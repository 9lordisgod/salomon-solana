# Product Scope

## Audience

Crypto-native users on Solana Mobile (Seeker / dApp Store) who want private on-device AI research and safe, user-approved on-chain actions. Sibling pure-AI product: [Salomon](https://github.com/9lordisgod/salomon) (no crypto hero there).

## In scope (goal — not shipped)

- Private research asks designed to run on-device by default.
- Propose-only on-chain actions that would require your explicit Seed Vault approval.

## Out of scope / not claimed

- No live Seed Vault integration or working chain actions yet.
- No autonomous signing, custody, or key handling by the model, ever.
- No silent cloud fallback for sensitive asks.
- No dApp Store listing, on-phone model, or mobile app release claimed as shipped.
