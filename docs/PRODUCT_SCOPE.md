# Product Scope

## Audience

Solana Mobile and Seeker users, such as traders, researchers, and journalists, who want private on-device AI research and safe, user-approved on-chain actions.

## In scope (goal)

- Private research asks answered on-device.
- Propose-only on-chain actions that you approve in Seed Vault.

## Out of scope / not claimed

- We do not claim a live Seed Vault integration or any working chain actions yet.
- No autonomous signing, custody, or key handling by the model, ever.
- No cloud fallback for sensitive asks.
- No dApp Store listing yet.
