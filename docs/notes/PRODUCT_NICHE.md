# Product niche — Salomon Solana

> Locked draft 2026-10-29 (America/Vancouver) · Solana Product · tip `002f34e`
> Parallel to pure-AI Salomon. Keep crypto/on-chain narrative here only.

## Primary

Crypto-native users on Solana Mobile (**Seeker / dApp Store**) who want on-device AI research, notes kept local by default, and keys never in the model.

## Distribution

Primary path: Solana Mobile / Seeker via dApp Store (goal — **not listed yet**).
Companions: none required for M0–M1; web companion is owned by pure-AI Salomon, not this hero.

## Promise (direction — not all shipped)

- Local-first / on-device by default; fail closed when no local model is connected.
- Notes and chat stay on the device unless the user opts into a labeled remote path (never silent; never default).
- Credentials and seed material stay out of the model; signatures are **intended** to go through Seed Vault (integration not shipped).
- Optional light Solana actions — user-explicit only (propose ≠ act).

## Foundation

Privacy / fail-closed stack shared in spirit with Salomon: local ask path, refusal gates, sanitizer-oriented design. Remote and on-chain are opt-in and labeled. Do not claim “local-only” as absolute while labeled REMOTE exists.

## Secondary / historical

Journalist / source-protection research framing informed early privacy work across the family of products; it is **not** the hero for this repo.

## Out of scope for hero claims (until built + honest)

Live Seed Vault signing; working chain actions; dApp Store listing as live fact; on-device phone model as a shipping product; attested remote as default; BYOK as silent fallback; wallet/DeFi/auto-trading agents; silent chain calls; any present-tense claim that implies the above shipped.
